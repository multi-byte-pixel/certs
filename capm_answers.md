### Question 1
**Correct answer:** A
**Why:** When the sponsor decides to terminate the project, the project manager should close the project rather than trying to convince the sponsor otherwise or insist on continuing delivery.

### Question 2
**Correct answer:** C
**Why:** Story mapping is the technique used to prioritize and organize features, epics, and user stories by business value, unlike story slicing (splitting stories) or use case diagrams (system interactions).

### Question 3
**Correct answer:** D
**Why:** A use case diagram is used to display the interactions between users (actors) and a system to accomplish a specific goal, which is different from a story map's purpose of sequencing by business value.

### Question 4

**Why:** Since the stakeholder structure changed significantly (sponsor replaced by steering committee), the project manager should acknowledge this change and tailor communication accordingly, which is broader and more proactive than just holding a meeting or sending a status report.

### Question 5

**Why:** Completing scope tasks or fixing defects isn't enough to avoid liabilities; the project manager must ensure all closing formalities were followed per the defined contractual procedures.

### Question 6
**Correct answer:** C
**Why:** Since work packages and constraints are already defined and top management wants a precise estimate, bottom-up estimating (estimating from detailed work packages upward) provides the most accurate result, unlike top-down/analogous methods.

### Question 7
**Correct answer:** B
**Why:** Opportunities are positive risks (as opposed to threats, which are negative risks), so a market opportunity should be categorized as a positive risk.

### Question 8

**Why:** After identifying defects for a Pareto analysis, the next step is to find out the frequency of each defect, since the Pareto chart ranks issues by frequency to reveal the top 20% causing 80% of problems.

### Question 9

### Question 11
